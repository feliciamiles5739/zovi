The guide

Fit and fabric

Measure a garment, not yourself. Lay a shirt that fits face down, buttons done up, tape armhole to armhole, double it, and order that chest size. A trouser goes the same way across the waistband. We cut one leg length, thirty-two inches.

Tees and polos run S to 2XL, cut straight: take the smaller size in jersey, the larger in pique. Shirts go by chest, 38 to 46 inches; trousers by waist. Every figure comes off a finished garment, not the pattern.

Care: cold water, inside out for anything dark, dry in shade, never tumble the pique. Unsure of a size? Send flat measurements with your order request and we will name the size.

Ordering, delivery and returns

How ordering works

One: fill the bag. Your browser keeps it; it never reaches us. Two: send it as an order request. The form emails the bag as a plain list with your name, address and sizes. Nothing is charged when you press send; there is no payment step here.

Three: we reply by hand, usually that evening, never past the second working day, naming what is on the rack, what awaits the next cutting run, and the total with delivery at cost. Four: you confirm, then pay the shop directly, off this website.

Delivery within India only, two to seven working days. Returns within ten days of delivery, unworn, unwashed, tags on.
